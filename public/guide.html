📋 Election Process Guide

Everything you need to know about voting in India — step by step

📝 Voter Registration

  • You must be an Indian citizen aged 18 or above on the qualifying date
  • Register online at voters.eci.gov.in or through Form 6 at your local ERO office
  • You will receive an EPIC (Voter ID) card with a unique 10-character alphanumeric code
  • Check your name in the electoral roll before election day
💡 Tip: You can check your registration status by entering your EPIC number on the ECI portal anytime.

🏘️ Know Your Constituency

  • India is divided into 543 Parliamentary and ~4000 Assembly constituencies
  • Your constituency is determined by your registered address
  • Each constituency has multiple polling booths — you are assigned to a specific one
  • You can only vote in your assigned constituency
💡 Tip: Use the "Find Booth" feature in Vote Saarthi to locate your assigned polling station.

📄 Election Day Preparation

  • Carry your Voter ID (EPIC card) — it is the primary identification document
  • Alternative IDs accepted: Aadhaar, Passport, Driving License, PAN Card
  • Polling hours are typically 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM (may vary by state)
  • Wearing or carrying party symbols/material is prohibited near polling stations
  • Employers must give paid leave on polling day — it's your legal right
💡 Tip: Vote early in the morning to avoid long queues. Check crowd levels on our Booth Finder!

🏛️ At the Polling Station

  • Join the queue — separate lines may exist for men, women, and senior citizens
  • Show your Voter ID to the polling officer at the entry desk
  • Your name is verified against the electoral roll
  • Indelible ink is applied to your left index finger
  • You receive a slip and proceed to the voting compartment

🖥️ Using the EVM & VVPAT

  • EVM (Electronic Voting Machine) displays candidate names, symbols, and party names
  • Press the blue button next to your chosen candidate
  • A beep confirms your vote, and a light glows next to the selected candidate
  • VVPAT (Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail) shows a slip for 7 seconds with your choice
  • The slip drops into a sealed box for verification if needed
💡 Tip: VVPAT lets you verify that your vote was recorded correctly. Check the slip before leaving!

✅ After Voting

  • The ink mark on your finger stays for about 2-3 weeks
  • Exit the polling station — do not discuss your vote inside
  • Results are typically announced within a few days after counting
  • You can track results on the ECI website or news channels

⚖️ Your Rights as a Voter

  • Right to vote by secret ballot — no one can see your choice
  • Right to NOTA — you can choose "None of the Above" if unsatisfied
  • Right to paid leave on election day from your employer
  • Right to complain about malpractice to the Election Commission
  • Right to accessibility — polling stations must be accessible to persons with disabilities
  • No one can be denied entry to a polling station based on caste, religion, or gender
